## Payment and HMO
- Cash, GCash, Maya, and all major credit cards.
- 0% installment for braces and implants (6 or 12 months) with BPI and Metrobank cards.
- HMO accepted: Maxicare, Intellicare, Medicard, PhilCare. Patients should bring their HMO card and a valid ID. HMO usually covers consultation, cleaning, simple extraction and fillings; cosmetic work is not covered.

## Policies
- New patients should arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a medical history form.
- Please reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment.
- Kids are welcome from age 3. A parent or guardian must stay during the visit.

## Emergencies
- During clinic hours: same-day emergency slots are kept open. Book the earliest slot or call (032) 555-0142.
- Severe swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding that won't stop, or facial trauma: go to the nearest hospital emergency room or call 911 right away.
